## Current work at Perplexity

I'm a Member of Technical Staff at @perplexity_ai, working at the intersection of search and AI. I built a small research team at Perplexity Berlin (https://www.perplexity.ai/hub/careers/berlin). Hiring is currently paused, with rare exceptions for high-profile candidates. My current work includes:

- citras: A high-performance representation learning framework for training dense, late-interaction, and multimodal representation models.
- pplx-embed-v1: Diffusion-Pretrained Dense and Contextual Embeddings. [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.11151) [blog](https://www.perplexity.ai/en-GB/hub/blog/pplx-embed-state-of-the-art-embedding-models-for-web-scale-retrieval)
- context pruner: Query-Aware Context Compression for Better Snippets. [blog](https://www.perplexity.ai/en-GB/hub/blog/query-aware-context-compression-for-better-snippets)
- q2d-web: A Large-Scale Benchmark for Retrieval in Agentic RAG Systems. [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.08887) [blog](https://www.perplexity.ai/en-GB/hub/blog/q2d-web)
- pplx-embed-v2-late: Multimodal embeddings beyond a single vector. [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.29523) [blog](https://www.perplexity.ai/en-GB/hub/blog/multimodal-embeddings-beyond-a-single-vector)
- pplx-embed-v2-context: Contextual Embeddings Beyond the Gold Chunk. [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.38854) [blog](https://www.perplexity.ai/en-GB/hub/blog/contextual-embedding-beyond-the-gold-passage)
- decider: Perplexity Decider v1.3: Training, eval, and use cases. [blog](https://www.perplexity.ai/en-GB/hub/blog/perplexity-decider)
- search quality: Supporting the team's efforts to improve search quality across the stack.
- continued pre-training and multimodal pre-training: Mining high-quality training corpora for Perplexity.
